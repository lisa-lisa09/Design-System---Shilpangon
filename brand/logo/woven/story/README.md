Annotated logo-meaning boards, in the style of an "identity explained" poster. The editable source is Figma: Design System v1 → Logo page → section **11 · Logo story**. The symbol and wordmark on the boards are linked instances of the approved masters.

## What the name means

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **শিল্প** (shilpo) | art, craft, skilled handiwork |
| **অঙ্গন** (angon) | courtyard, open yard (আঙিনা / উঠান); also an arena or field, as in ক্রীড়াঙ্গন |
| **শিল্প + অঙ্গন → শিল্পাঙ্গন** | স্বরসন্ধি: অ + অ = আ |

**শিল্পাঙ্গন = "the courtyard of craft" (শিল্পের অঙ্গন):** a shared place where women artisans make, show and sell their work.

## What the mark means (designer's intended symbolism)

1. **One unbroken thread:** the whole mark is a single continuous line, like handwork that is woven or stitched without a break.
2. **Over and under:** at every crossing one strand holds the other, the way makers, buyers and community support each other.
3. **Four loops:** many makers and crafts reaching outward from one shared centre.
4. **The centre diamond:** the অঙ্গন itself, the open courtyard where everyone meets, which is the marketplace.
5. **Wordmark:** Bangla first, Baloo Da 2 SemiBold; the conjuncts ল্প and ঙ্গ stay intact.
6. **Colour:** brick red #B8342B (Pohela Boishakh red, terracotta, Halkhata). See `../color-rationale.md`.

These are intended meanings, not historical or research claims.
